All six music packs are being prepared for launch across itch.io, Unity Asset Store, and FAB. Each pack ships in three formats — WAV, OGG Vorbis, and MP3 — with 40-second demo previews included. Pricing is $80 per pack.

All tracks are full-length compositions (5–5.5 minutes each), not loops, composed in G# minor for seamless in-game mixing. Created using AIVA with full copyright ownership transferred to Crimson Lantern Studios.

itch.io goes live first. Unity and FAB listings are submitted for review.
