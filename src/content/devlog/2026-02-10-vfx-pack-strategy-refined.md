## New Strategy

Each pack will showcase our techno-arcane aesthetic — hand-painted LoRA-trained effects that blur the line between magic and technology.

Organizing by magical school gives each pack a distinct identity while maintaining the cohesive Crimson Lantern visual language across the collection.
