Three interconnected music packs totaling 60 tracks are ready for launch on FAB and Unity Asset Store.

## The Three Packs

- **Disturbingly Beautiful** — Ambient synthwave. Impossible to categorize. Deeply compelling.
- **Blood & Adrenaline** — Combat music. Liquid DnB meets dystopian intensity.
- **Rise To It** — Boss battle anthology. Angelic metal meets hypnotic fury.

All composed in our signature G# minor key, these packs form **The Crimson Collection** — 60 tracks spanning three distinct worlds of sound.
