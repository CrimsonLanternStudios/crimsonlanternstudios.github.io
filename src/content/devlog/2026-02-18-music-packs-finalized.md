Six interconnected music packs totaling 120 tracks are ready for launch on itch.io, Unity Asset Store, and FAB.

The **Disturbingly Beautiful** ambient collection, **Blood & Adrenaline** combat suite, and **Rise To It** boss battle anthology — plus **Hearthstone & Cobble** town BGM, **Wanderer's Rest** exploration BGM, and **Thin Air** tension & dialog scoring — all in our signature G# minor.

Each pack contains 20 tracks designed to work both independently and as part of the larger Crimson Collection. The packs will be available individually at $80 each, or as a complete bundle for $200.
