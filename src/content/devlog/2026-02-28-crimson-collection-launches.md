The full Crimson Collection is live on itch.io — six music packs totaling 120 original compositions and over 10 hours of soundtrack.

**Disturbingly Beautiful** (ambient), **Blood & Adrenaline** (combat), **Rise To It** (boss battles), **Hearthstone & Cobble** (town BGM), **Wanderer's Rest** (exploration BGM), and **Thin Air** (tension & dialog).

All tracks in G# minor, all commercially licensed, all stream-safe. Unity Asset Store and FAB listings to follow.
